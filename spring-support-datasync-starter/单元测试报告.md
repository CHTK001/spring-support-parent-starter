```
[APP] plugin.datasync.mapping-source=file
[APP] plugin.datasync.file-mapping.directory-path=./src/test/resources/test-mappings

```

```
[APP-DB] plugin.datasync.enabled=true
[APP-DB] plugin.datasync.mapping-source=database
[APP-DB] plugin.datasync.database-mapping.enabled=true
[APP-DB] plugin.datasync.database-mapping.table-prefix=datasync_
[APP-DB] plugin.datasync.database-mapping.auto-init-table=true

DataSync 表结构初始化完成: datasync_mapping, datasync_field_mapping
数据库加载映射: mappingId=db-user-sync
```
